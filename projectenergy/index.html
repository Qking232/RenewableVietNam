      Data Sources & Transparency
      1. Overpass API (Live)

      Real-time community-maintained geospatial data for Vietnamese power infrastructure (power=plant). Free, keyless live REST endpoint.

      2. Global Energy Monitor (GEM)

      Global Solar, Wind, and Hydropower Trackers providing verified MW capacities, asset ownership, and grid status.

      3. National Power Development Plan VIII (PDP8)

      Vietnam Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT) master energy framework for utility-scale transmission corridors and additions through 2030–2050.

      4. Open Development Mekong (ODM)

      Geospatial database of Lower Mekong hydropower dams, power lines, and renewable concessions.
