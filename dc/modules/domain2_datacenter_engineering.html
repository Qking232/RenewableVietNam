3. Thermal Management, Chiller Plant & Direct Liquid Cooling (DLC)

Hybrid high-efficiency cooling combining oil-free magnetic chillers with direct-to-chip liquid cooling loops for high-density AI clusters.

Magnetic-Bearing Chillers (N+2)

6 units of 2,500 RT water-cooled centrifugal chillers equipped with frictionless magnetic bearings. Part-load efficiency NPLV ≤ 0.34 kW/RT (COP ≥ 10.3). Fast-restart capability restores 80% cooling capacity within 90 seconds of power resumption.

Total Plant: 15,000 RT (N+2 Redundant)
Direct-to-Chip Liquid Cooling (DLC)

Dedicated In-Row Coolant Distribution Units (CDUs) circulating treated deionized water-glycol fluid directly to CPU and GPU micro-channel cold plates. Capable of rejecting 85% of heat from 80 kW/rack AI clusters directly into liquid loops with supply water at 32°C.

Secondary Loop: 1.91 L/s per Rack
Waterside Free Cooling Economizer

Plate heat exchangers installed parallel to chillers bypass mechanical compressors whenever ambient wet bulb drops ≤ 22°C. Enables over 1,800 equivalent full-load hours of free or partial-economizer cooling annually in southern Vietnam winter/monsoon conditions.

Energy Saving: 2,400,000 kWh / Year
