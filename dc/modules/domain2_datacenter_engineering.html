2. Standby Gensets
SYS-GEN-11KV 		Provides continuous islanded prime electrical generation during total utility grid blackout, severe voltage depression, or catastrophic substation outage. Automatic Mains Failure (AMF) controllers detect grid voltage loss (<90% for 1.5s). 3. 2N Static UPS & BESS
SYS-UPS-2N 		Eliminates all utility power disturbances (sags, swells, transients, harmonics) and supplies uninterrupted synthetic AC power (0ms transfer) to white space compute and critical pumps. Continuous online double conversion: Incoming AC is rectified to 540V DC; high-efficiency IGBT inverters regenerate pure 400V sine-wave AC. During utility loss, LiFePO4 battery strings instantly supply DC bus without breaker actuation or transfer delay. 15x 2,000 kVA (System A)

Maintenance bypass isolator (Q3) allows complete de-energization for safe servicing.
4. Overhead Track Busway
SYS-BUSWAY-400V 		Transports low-voltage 3-phase power directly above server rack aisles, providing flexible, hot-swappable drop connections to rack power distribution units (rPDUs). Continuous extruded copper busbars encased in an aluminum track housing hang overhead. Plug-in tap-off boxes connect mechanically and electrically with twist-and-lock safety interlocks while busway remains energized. 800A • 400V 3P4W

50kA / 1s Fault Rating 		Dual A+B Overhead Rails: Track A fed from UPS-A, Track B fed from UPS-B. Dual-corded server power supplies (PSUs) receive redundant power feeds at every rack footprint. Busway vs Circuit Breaker: Busway replaces rigid conduits and floor cable clutter. Overcurrent circuit breakers (63A/32A MCB/MCCB) reside inside each tap-off box to isolate localized rack faults.
5. Water-Cooled Chillers
SYS-CHILL-WTR 		Produces centralized chilled water (CHW at 12.0°C supply) to absorb heat dissipated by computing servers, CRAH units, and liquid cooling CDUs across the data hall. Variable-speed centrifugal chillers compress HFO-1233zd(E) low-GWP refrigerant. Chilled water circulates through plant loops while heat is rejected via condenser water loops to rooftop induced-draft cooling towers. 6x 1,500 RT Chillers (9,000 RT)
4,500 m³ TES Tank
12.0°C Supply / 18.0°C Return 		N+2 Chiller Plant Redundancy: Four chillers satisfy 100% peak design heat load; two chillers remain on hot standby. Stratified TES tank guarantees 15 minutes of uninterrupted thermal ride-through during generator startup. Variable primary flow (VPF) with automated differential pressure bypass control valves. Chiller plant controller sequences compressors to maintain optimal coefficient of performance (COP ≥ 6.4).
6. CRAH Fan Arrays & HAC
SYS-CRAH-AIR 		Controls white space air temperature, humidity, and airflow velocity, ensuring server intake temperatures conform strictly to ASHRAE A1 specifications (18°C–27°C). Electronically commutated (EC) fans pull warm air (35°C–40°C) from contained hot aisles through cooling coils fed with chilled water, discharging pressurized cold air (20°C–22°C) under the raised floor or down flooded cold aisles. 36x 180 kW Downflow Units

Physical Hot-Aisle Containment (HAC) with double-glazed sliding doors eliminates thermal mixing, enabling elevated chilled water supply temperatures and maximizing chiller efficiency.
7. Direct Liquid Cooling (DLC)
SYS-DLC-CDU 		Dissipates ultra-dense thermal loads directly at the GPU/CPU silicon die for high-performance AI accelerator compute clusters (> 50 kW to 120 kW per rack).
