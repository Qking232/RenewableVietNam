3. Data Center Fire Safety & Clean Agent System Architecture (QCVN 06:2022 & NFPA 75/2001)

Dual-barrier protection combining aspirating smoke detection, clean gaseous total flooding, and double-interlock pre-action sprinklers.

Aspiration Smoke Detection (VESDA)

Dual-chamber laser particle counters installed in both return air plenums and server cold/hot aisles. 4 alarm thresholds: Alert (0.005% obs/m), Action (0.015%), Fire 1 (0.04%), Fire 2 (0.08%). Guarantees warning prior to visible flame inception.

Sampling Pipe Grid: 1 hole per 25 m² area
Clean Agent Gas Flooding (FK-5-1-12)

Fluoroketone FK-5-1-12 charged at 50 bar. Zero Ozone Depletion Potential (ODP = 0) and Global Warming Potential (GWP ≤ 1). Total design flooding concentration 5.6% vol deployed within ≤ 10 seconds. Electrically non-conductive, non-corrosive, safe for energized electronics.

Design Volume: 6,608 kg / Hall
Double-Interlock Pre-Action Sprinkler

Secondary fail-safe mechanism under NFPA 13 and TCVN 3890. Piping network pressurized with nitrogen (1.5 bar). Requires BOTH cross-zoned smoke detector actuation AND thermal glass bulb burst (68°C) before water enters piping, preventing accidental discharge on energized IT racks.

Water Supply: Dedicated 750 m³ 3-hr concrete cistern

4. Environmental Protection License (GPMT) & Acoustic / Exhaust Abatement

Statutory compliance under Decree 08/2022/NĐ-CP governing 10 x 3,000 kVA emergency diesel generators and bulk fuel management.

Generator Flue Gas & Dispersion Modeling

10 units of 3,000 kVA standby generators produce exhaust gases during monthly testing and grid outages. Vertical stacks discharge at +28m above roof level. Aerodynamic AERMOD dispersion modeling proves ground-level concentrations of SO₂, NOₓ, and CO comply with QCVN 05:2023/BTNM (Ambient Air Quality) and QCVN 19:2009/BTNMT (Industrial Emission).

Total Fuel Storage: 150,000 Litres (48h Reserve)
Acoustic Attenuation & Noise Abatement

Unattenuated 3,000 kVA generator engine noise reaches 108 dBA at 1m. Generator enclosures utilize custom acoustic splitters, heavy-density rockwool lining, and critical-grade silencers achieving 35 dBA insertion loss. Boundary fence noise levels modeled at ≤ 65 dBA daytime and ≤ 55 dBA nighttime, complying with QCVN 26:2010/BTNMT.

Boundary Sound Pressure: 53.9 dBA (Compliant)
