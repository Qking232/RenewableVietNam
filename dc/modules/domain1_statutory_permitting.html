Data Center Fire Safety Engineering Architecture (QCVN 06:2022/BXD & NFPA 75/2001)

Comprehensive life safety, passive fire containment, aspirating smoke detection, and clean agent gas flooding systems.

ZERO DISCHARGE IT DAMAGE
Passive Fire Compartmentalization (REI 180) 3-HOUR BARRIER
  • Cast-in-Place Concrete Walls: 250mm reinforced concrete shear walls with 3-hour fire rating (REI 180) separating all critical data halls, battery rooms, and 22kV switchgear galleries.
  • Auto-Closing Fire Doors: Class A fire-rated doors (EI 90) equipped with electromagnetic hold-open devices and positive latching upon smoke alarm activation.
  • Cable & Pipe Penetrations: 100% of cable trays and busway penetrations sealed with UL-listed intumescent firestop pillows, elastomeric firestop caulks, and ablative coated boards.
  • Overpressure Relief Dampers: Gravity-operated overpressure dampers calibrated to prevent structural wall cracking during instantaneous 10-second gas discharge.
FK-5-1-12 Total Clean Agent Gas Flooding NOVEC 1230 EQUIV
  • Zero Environmental Footprint: FK-5-1-12 (dodecafluoro-2-methylpentan-3-one) clean agent with Ozone Depletion Potential ODP = 0.00 and Global Warming Potential GWP < 1.
  • Design Concentration: Engineered to 5.8% design concentration (NOAEL = 10.0%), providing an ample safety margin for operational personnel egress.
  • 10-Second Rapid Discharge: High-pressure nitrogen-boosted delivery discharging 1,425 kg of agent across engineered nozzles in under 10 seconds to snuff combustion instantly.
  • Zero Conductive Residue: Electrically non-conductive, evaporates cleanly without moisture or chemical residue, preventing hardware corrosion or server downtime.
Aspirating Very Early Warning Detection (VESDA) LASERPLUS SYSTEM
  • Continuous Air Sampling: Laser-based aspirating air sampling continuously drawing air from hot and cold aisles with an air transit time ≤ 60 seconds.
  • 4-Stage Multi-Threshold Alerting: Progressive sensitivity settings: Alert (0.015% obs/m), Action (0.03% obs/m), Fire 1 (0.08% obs/m), and Fire 2 (0.20% obs/m).
  • Cross-Zone Coincidence: Automatic gas discharge requires dual-zone coincidence (VESDA Stage 4 + addressable optical smoke detector) plus 30-second abort delay.
  • Integrated HVAC Shutdown: Direct interface with Building Management System (BMS) to close motorized fire dampers and isolate data hall air circulation prior to release.
Double-Interlock Pre-Action Water Sprinkler FAIL-SAFE SECONDARY
  • Dry Pipe Architecture: Sprinkler pipes remain dry and pressurized with supervisory nitrogen/air until an electric fire alarm signal AND sprinkler head bulb rupture occur simultaneously.
  • Zero Accidental Water Leaks: Mechanical pipe damage alone cannot trigger water flow into critical data halls, preventing catastrophic server short-circuits.
  • Dedicated Water Supply: 3,600m³ underground reinforced concrete fire water retention tank with 100% redundant diesel and electric fire booster pumps (4-hour continuous supply).
  • Smoke Spill Exhaust: Mechanical smoke evacuation fans rated for 10 air changes per hour (ACH) to purge toxic post-fire pyrolysis gases safely to atmosphere.
