1. The "5+3" Capital Delivery System Architecture

Enterprise governance standard bridging financial capital authorization, multi-agency statutory licensing, and 5-level commissioning.

The "5" Statutory & Physical Pillars

Encompasses the sequential physical realization stream: (1) Land Acquisition & 1/500 Master Plan, (2) TKCS Basic Design & Fire Safety (PCCC) Approval, (3) Phased Dual-Track Construction Permitting (GPXD Móng & Thân), (4) High-Voltage 110kV GIS Substation & MEP Fit-out, and (5) 5-Level Commissioning (Cx 1-5) & Decree 06/2021 Statutory Hoàn Công.

The "+3" Institutional Control Vectors

Maintains continuous enterprise cross-functional oversight: (+1) Earned Value Management (EVM) Financial Controls via monthly S-Curve progress auditing, (+2) FIDIC Silver Book EPC Turnkey Risk Allocation with strict PUE performance liquidated damages, and (+3) Tier III Concurrent Maintainability Verification ensuring zero operational interruption during routine servicing.
