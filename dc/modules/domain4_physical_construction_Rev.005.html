Pillar 1: Structural & Load-Bearing Dynamics

TCVN 9386 / ACI 318

Data halls are engineered with post-tensioned reinforced concrete slabs designed to carry 25.0 kN/m² dynamic live loads without exceeding L/600 deflection. Columns are arranged in a 9m x 9m grid aligning with hot/cold aisle geometry to prevent structural obstructions.

DC-Exclusive Construction Features:
  • Vibration-Isolated Inertia Bases: Heavy concrete plinths on spring snubbers preventing generator harmonic resonance from transmitting to disk drives.
  • Mass Raft Thermal Control: 1.8m thick slab with low-heat slag cement; thermocouple arrays ensure core-to-surface ΔT ≤ 20°C.
  • Continuous Floor Hardening: Metallic dry-shake floor hardener applied monolithically to eliminate concrete dusting under raised floors.

Pillar 2: Thermal Barrier & Airflow Architecture

ASHRAE TC 9.9

The white space envelope forms an airtight, insulated thermal cocoon. 800mm raised flooring creates a high-pressure underfloor cold supply plenum, while Hot Aisle Containment (HAC) baffles route 45°C hot server exhaust directly to CRAC returns without air mixing.

DC-Exclusive Construction Features:
  • HAC Rigid Ceiling Baffles: Flame-retardant clear polycarbonate panels with magnetic hold-opens that drop on VESDA alarm for fire clearance.
  • Plenum Pressure Sealing: All slab penetrations sealed with silicone firestop gaskets; plenum pressure tested to ≤0.05 CFM/ft² at 25 Pa.
  • Windowless Vapor Shell: Continuous 0.10 perm interior vapor retarder eliminating ambient tropical moisture infiltration.

Pillar 3: Dual Diverse Ingress & Yard Civil

TIA-942-B Tier IV

Eliminates all single points of physical failure before utility services enter the building. Fiber paths, medium-voltage power feeders, and backup fuel piping enter through geographically separated perimeter points with independent physical containment.

DC-Exclusive Construction Features:
  • Dual Diverse MMRs: MMR-A on North perimeter, MMR-B on South perimeter separated by ≥30m with zero shared trenching.
  • 16-Way Encased Duct Banks: Heavy reinforced concrete with red oxide dye at -1.8m depth to prevent excavation strikes.
  • 110% Transformer Bunds: Concrete catchment basins under dual 40 MVA units with gravity oil-water separation tanks.

Pillar 4: Specialized Fire & Environmental Safety

NFPA 75 / ISO 14644

Data halls require dual-action fire protection to prevent catastrophic water damage from pipe leaks. Clean agents suppress electrical fires, while double-interlock pre-action systems ensure pipes remain completely dry until both smoke and heat sensors confirm a fire.

DC-Exclusive Construction Features:
  • Pre-Action Double-Interlock Sprinklers: Dry-pipe network requiring simultaneous VESDA laser detection + thermal bulb burst before admitting water.
  • Clean Agent Gaseous Flooding: IG-541 inert gas in battery/switchgear rooms with automated pressure relief dampers.
  • Zero Zinc-Whisker Protocol: Strict contractual ban on electroplated zinc hardware (ASTM B633); hot-dip galvanized or passivated nickel only.
