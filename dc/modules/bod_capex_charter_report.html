1. Strategic Investment Mandate & APAC Hyperscale Market Drivers

Macroeconomic and geopolitical catalysts driving hyperscale colocation demand across Southeast Asia and Vietnam's decisive regional competitive positioning.

AI Compute & Hyperscale Migration

Global cloud providers and AI foundation model developers are shifting workloads to Vietnam due to data sovereignty mandates (Decree 53/2022/NĐ-CP) and competitive power costs ($0.082/kWh vs $0.22/kWh in Singapore and $0.16/kWh in Tokyo).

Market CAGR: 19.8% (2024–2030)
Subsea Fiber Interconnection Hub

Direct low-latency dark fiber access to next-generation international subsea cables (ADC, SJC2, APG, AAG) provides sub-30ms round-trip latency to Singapore, Hong Kong, and Tokyo, establishing carrier-neutral connectivity.

Subsea Gateways: 4 Direct Landings
Green Energy Advantage (DPPA)

Adoption of Decree 80/2024 Direct Power Purchase Agreement allows corporate off-takers to secure 100% verified renewable energy (wind/solar) directly from Ninh Thuan / Binh Thuan generation assets, satisfying global ESG mandates.

DRAFT BOARD RESOLUTION NO. BOD-RES-2026-DC01

WHEREAS, the Executive Leadership Team has presented a fully engineered, de-risked capital proposal for the development and construction of a 45 MW critical IT capacity Hyperscale Data Center Campus in Vietnam;

WHEREAS, the Board Investment Committee has rigorously audited the financial return model, confirming a base case unlevered Internal Rate of Return of 18.24% and a Net Present Value of $42,820,000 USD at a 10.0% discount rate;

WHEREAS, all statutory regulatory dependencies, including Decree 15/2021 Construction Permitting, QCVN 06:2022 Fire Safety compliance, Decree 08/2022 Environmental Licensing, and EVN 110kV grid connection agreements have been de-risked via dual-track execution architecture;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT:

  1. The Board hereby authorizes the total capital expenditure budget of $180,000,000 USD for the complete turnkey delivery of the Vietnam Hyperscale Data Center Campus.
  2. The Project Director and Chief Financial Officer are authorized to execute the FIDIC Silver Book EPC Turnkey Contract with the selected Tier 1 EPC Contractor, subject to standard performance guarantees and liquidated damages protections.
  3. The Executive Team is authorized to enter into binding Direct Power Purchase Agreements (DPPA) for 100% renewable power off-take and finalize the 60 MVA firm grid connection with EVN.
  4. Capital disbursements shall be governed strictly by the 6 Stage Gate Capital Drawdown authorization protocol, with each tranche contingent on documented milestone verification.
  5. Execution progress shall be monitored monthly via Earned Value Management (EVM) S-Curve reporting to the Board Investment Committee.
