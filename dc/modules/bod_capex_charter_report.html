1. Strategic Investment Rationale & APAC Hyperscale Market Context

Macroeconomic catalysts driving hyperscale colocation demand across Southeast Asia and Vietnam's competitive positioning.

AI Compute & Hyperscale Migration

Global cloud providers and AI foundation model developers are shifting workloads to Vietnam due to data sovereignty mandates (Decree 53/2022/NĐ-CP) and competitive power costs ($0.082/kWh vs $0.22/kWh in Singapore).

Market CAGR: 19.8% (2024–2030)
Subsea Fiber Interconnection Hub

Direct low-latency dark fiber access to next-generation international subsea cables (ADC, SJC2, APG) provides sub-30ms round-trip latency to Singapore, Hong Kong, and Tokyo, establishing regional carrier neutrality.

Subsea Gateways: 4 Direct Landings
Green Energy Advantage (DPPA)

Adoption of Decree 80/2024 Direct Power Purchase Agreement allows corporate off-takers to secure 100% verified renewable energy (wind/solar) directly from Ninh Thuan / Binh Thuan generation assets, satisfying global ESG mandates.

Scope 2 Emissions: 100% Net Zero

4. Formal Board of Directors Capital Resolution & Execution Authorization

Formal institutional governance text for executive adoption and execution.

RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS • CAPITAL EXPENDITURE AUTHORIZATION

WHEREAS, the Company has evaluated the commercial, technical, and statutory feasibility of developing a 45 MW critical IT capacity Tier III / Tier IV carrier-neutral hyperscale data center facility in Vietnam; and

WHEREAS, the Executive Management Team has presented the audited Capital Budget at Completion (BAC) of $180,000,000 USD, supported by a 15-year Discounted Cash Flow valuation yielding a Net Present Value of $42,820,000 USD and an Unlevered Internal Rate of Return of 18.24%; and

WHEREAS, the statutory permitting sequence, QCVN 06:2022 Fire Safety compliance, Decree 08/2022 Environmental Licensing, and EVN 110kV grid connection agreements have been de-risked via dual-track execution architecture;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT:

  1. The Board hereby authorizes the total capital expenditure budget of $180,000,000 USD for the complete turnkey delivery of the Vietnam Hyperscale Data Center Campus.
  2. The Project Director and Chief Financial Officer are authorized to execute the FIDIC Silver Book EPC Turnkey Contract with the selected Tier 1 EPC Contractor, subject to standard performance guarantees and liquidated damages protections.
  3. The Executive Team is authorized to enter into binding Direct Power Purchase Agreements (DPPA) for 100% renewable power off-take and finalize the 60 MVA firm grid connection with EVN.
  4. Execution progress shall be monitored monthly via Earned Value Management (EVM) S-Curve reporting to the Board Investment Committee.
