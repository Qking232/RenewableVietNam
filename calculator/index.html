VIETNAM GREEN GROWTH COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES

Under Vietnam's National Green Growth Strategy (Decision 1658/QD-TTg) and PDP8 (Decision 500/QD-TTg), Vietnam delivers significant institutional advantages for multinational exporters targeting European markets:

  • ~30% Clean Hydro Base Generation: Vietnam's national transmission network incorporates large clean hydroelectric cascades, reducing grid lifecycle carbon intensity.
  • Statutory DPPA Wheeling (Decree 80/2024): Offsite wind and solar contracts allow manufacturers to achieve zero-carbon Scope 2 certification.
  • 168+ Eco-Industrial Parks (Decree 35/2022): Master-planned industrial clusters integrating rooftop solar, closed-loop water treatment, and LEED/LOTUS standards.
