        Where the library lives

        The public shelf is stored in Postgres (Supabase) and the files themselves in Supabase Storage. Submissions publish immediately — nothing is held back for review. To remove something, open Table Editor → hub_documents (or the hub-docs storage bucket) and delete the row or file.

        While the shared store is switched off, the documents you add and your notes live only in this browser (local storage), and files up to about 1 MB are kept inline.

        Reference sources used

        • Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT) & the National Power Development Plan (PDP8)
        • EVN / EVNNPT — national utility and grid operator disclosures
        • IEA, IRENA, World Bank, ADB, UNDP, ILO — energy, finance and just-transition analysis
        • Global Energy Monitor, Ember, Global Wind Energy Council — asset and generation trackers

        Collections

        Manage collections in the Browse Docs column: ＋ New collection adds one, ✎ renames it, and ✕ deletes it (any document using a deleted collection moves to another one automatically). A document's own collection can also be changed from the dropdown beside its number in the reader.

        Hidden documents & collections

        Deleting a built-in or public document hides it from your shelf in this browser, and deleting a built-in collection hides it. Bring them all back here.

        Reset

        Clears the documents and notes you have added in this browser. The built-in library is unaffected.
