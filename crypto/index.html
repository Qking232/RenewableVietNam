Quantitative Methodology & Risk Architecture

Market Regime Conditioning: Altcoin returns carry significant market beta (βBTC ≈ 1.2–1.8). In Risk-Off regimes (BTC below both 20d and 50d EMAs), long trades have historically negative expectancy. Trade defensive sizing or hold cash when the regime is red.

Horizon Volatility Sizing: Target and stop levels are dynamically calculated using 14-day Average True Range (ATR) scaled to √3 for a 3-day swing trade. Target is placed at 1.85 × σ3d and stop at 1.25 × σ3d to protect against intra-swing noise while securing an asymmetric ≥ 1.48 Risk:Reward ratio.

Not Financial Advice: Systematic quantitative models provide probabilistic edges, not certainties. Always enforce strict stop-loss discipline and never risk more than 1-2% of total trading equity on any single asset.
