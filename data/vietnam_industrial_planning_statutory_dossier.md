**Effective Legal Regime**: Post-Law on Planning (Law No. 21/2017/QH14)  
**Compilation Date**: October 2026

## Executive Summary & Regulatory Paradigm Shift

Under the pre-2018 legal regime (governed by the 2003 Land Law, 2003 Construction Law, and Decree No. 29/2008/NĐ-CP), Vietnam operated under a standalone **"National Master Plan for the Development of Industrial Parks"** approved periodically by the Prime Minister (e.g., Decision No. 1107/QĐ-TTg dated 21 August 2006).

**Critical Legal Paradigm Shift**: With the enactment and effective enforcement of the **Law on Planning 2017 (Law No. 21/2017/QH14)**, all standalone sector-specific industrial zone master plans were formally **abolished** (Article 59). 

Consequently, for the statutory horizon **2021–2030 (vision to 2050)**, official Government documents governing industrial parks (KCN), economic zones (KKT), and industrial clusters (CCN) are structured hierarchically across **five integrated statutory tiers**:

1. **National Level**: National Master Plan (Resolution No. 81/2023/QH15) & National Land-Use Master Plan (Decision No. 326/QĐ-TTg & Amending Decision No. 227/QĐ-TTg).
2. **Regulatory & Governance Framework**: Decree No. 35/2022/NĐ-CP on management of industrial parks and economic zones, supplemented by the draft Law on Industrial Parks and Economic Zones.
3. **Regional Level**: 6 Regional Socio-Economic Master Plans approved by the Prime Minister in 2022–2024 (Decisions 368, 370, 376, 369, 377, 287/QĐ-TTg).
4. **Provincial Level**: 63 Provincial Master Plans approved by the Prime Minister, each containing statutory annexes detailing the binding list, scale, and phase-in timeline for every IP/CCN through 2030/2050.
5. **Clean Energy, Decarbonization & Grid Linkage**: National Power Development Plan VIII (Decision No. 500/QĐ-TTg), Direct Power Purchase Agreements (Decree No. 80/2024/NĐ-CP), and Rooftop Solar for Industrial Complexes (Decree No. 135/2024/NĐ-CP).

## 1. National-Level Statutory Architecture

### 1.1 Law on Planning No. 21/2017/QH14
* **Promulgated**: 24 November 2017 by the National Assembly (XIV Session).
* **Effective Date**: 01 January 2019.
* **Core Impact on Industrial Zones**: 
  - Abolished fragmented ministerial/sectoral planning for industrial zones.
  - Mandated that the development plan of industrial zones within each province must be formulated as a dedicated component (*Phương án phát triển hệ thống khu công nghiệp*) inside the Provincial Master Plan, aligned with the National Land-Use Master Plan and Regional Master Plans.

### 1.2 Resolution No. 81/2023/QH15 (09/01/2023) – National Master Plan to 2030, Vision to 2050
* **Issuing Body**: National Assembly of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam (XV Session).
* **Title**: *Quy hoạch tổng thể quốc gia thời kỳ 2021 - 2030, tầm nhìn đến năm 2050*.
* **Statutory Directives for Industrial Zones**:
  - **Dynamic Growth Centers**: Concentrates mega-industrial clusters into two primary engines: Northern Dynamic Zone (Hanoi - Hai Phong - Quang Ninh - Bac Ninh - Hung Yen) and Southern Dynamic Zone (HCMC - Binh Duong - Dong Nai - Ba Ria Vung Tau).
  - **Industrial Economic Corridors**: Defines key industrial transport and logistics corridors: North-South Highway Corridor, Lao Cai - Hanoi - Hai Phong Corridor, Moc Bai - HCMC - Ba Ria Vung Tau Corridor, and the East-West Economic Corridors (Lao Bao - Da Nang, Cau Treo - Vung Ang).
  - **Sectoral Specialization**: Shifts coastal economic zones toward marine logistics, green metallurgy, and LNG-to-power petrochemicals; shifts inland hubs toward electronics, semiconductors, and specialized high-tech assembly.

## 2. National Land-Use Allocation Master Plan & Quotas

The physical establishment or expansion of any industrial zone in Vietnam is legally constrained by the statutory **Industrial Land Quota** (*Chỉ tiêu đất khu công nghiệp*) distributed from the central government down to each province.

### 2.1 Decision No. 326/QĐ-TTg (09/03/2022)
* **Signatory**: Prime Minister Pham Minh Chinh.
* **Title**: *Phân bổ chỉ tiêu Quy hoạch sử dụng đất quốc gia thời kỳ 2021 - 2030, tầm nhìn đến năm 2050, Kế hoạch sử dụng đất quốc gia 5 năm 2021 - 2025*.
* **Aggregate Industrial Land Allocation**:
  - Total statutory industrial zone land nationwide by 2030: **205,800 ha** (representing a net addition of >115,000 ha relative to the 2020 baseline).
  - Divided into 5-year interim ceilings (2021–2025) and 10-year horizons (2021–2030) for all 63 provinces.

### 2.2 Decision No. 227/QĐ-TTg (12/03/2024) – Statutory Land Quota Adjustment
* **Signatory**: Prime Minister Pham Minh Chinh.
* **Title**: *Điều chỉnh một số chỉ tiêu sử dụng đất đến năm 2025 được Thủ tướng Chính phủ phân bổ tại Quyết định số 326/QĐ-TTg*.
* **Key Mechanism**: Resolved quota exhaustion bottlenecks in hyper-growth FDI provinces. Reallocated unused quotas from slower-absorbing provinces to industrial epicenters:
  - Augmented industrial land ceilings for: Bac Ninh, Vinh Phuc, Hai Duong, Quang Ninh, Binh Dinh, Quang Nam, Dong Nai, Ba Ria - Vung Tau, and Long An.
  - Retained strict compliance with national ecological reserve ceilings and overall 2030 statutory envelope.

## 3. Dedicated Regulatory Framework: Decree No. 35/2022/NĐ-CP

* **Promulgated**: 28 May 2022 by the Government (effective 15 July 2022, replacing Decree No. 82/2018/NĐ-CP).
* **Title**: *Nghị định quy định về quản lý khu công nghiệp và khu kinh tế*.

### 3.1 Statutory Classification of Modern Industrial Parks
1. **Multi-sector Industrial Park** (*KCN đa ngành*): Standard multi-industry manufacturing hubs.
2. **Specialized Industrial Park** (*KCN chuyên sâu*): At least 60% of leasable industrial land dedicated to designated core sectors or integrated supply chains.
3. **Supporting Industrial Park** (*KCN hỗ trợ*): At least 60% of leasable industrial land allocated to enterprises producing supporting industry goods per Government Decree 111/2015/NĐ-CP.
4. **Eco-Industrial Park** (*KCN sinh thái*): Compliant with strict UNIDO/MPI circularity criteria; minimum 20% total land dedicated to green spaces and infrastructure; mandatory resource efficiency, clean production, and at least 1 verified industrial symbiosis linkage.
5. **High-tech Industrial Park** (*KCN công nghệ cao*): Dedicated to high-technology development, R&D, biotechnology, and semiconductor microelectronics.

### 3.2 Statutory Occupancy Gatekeepers for New IP Formation
* **The 60% Threshold (Article 9 & 10)**: To establish a new industrial park in a province, the existing operating industrial parks in that province must achieve an aggregate average occupancy rate of **at least 60%**.
* **Statutory Exemptions**: The 60% occupancy requirement is waived for:
  - Conversion or establishment of certified **Eco-Industrial Parks**.
  - Specialized high-tech, semiconductor, or supporting industry parks.
  - Industrial parks located within officially designated National Economic Zones (KKT).

### 3.3 Draft Law on Industrial Parks and Economic Zones
* **Lead Drafting Agency**: Ministry of Planning and Investment (MPI).
* **Objective**: Elevate the regulatory framework from a Government Decree to a parliamentary Law (*Luật Khu công nghiệp, Khu kinh tế*) to provide long-term statutory certainty, superior fiscal incentives, administrative autonomy for Management Boards (One-Stop-Shop authority), and legal sandboxes for circular economy models.

## 5. Provincial Master Plans: Statutory Industrial Zone Catalogs (Top 15 Hubs)

Every province in Vietnam has had its **Provincial Master Plan for 2021–2030, vision to 2050** approved by the Prime Minister. Each approval decision contains binding statutory Annexes listing:
- Total allocated industrial land quota.
- Specific list of existing IPs to be maintained or expanded.
- Specific list of newly planned IPs with designated land areas and geographic coordinates.

### Key Provincial Industrial Decisions:

1. **Bac Ninh**: **Decision No. 1588/QĐ-TTg** (08/12/2023)
   - *Target*: 24 Industrial Parks with total planned land area exceeding 8,200 ha; specialized semiconductor and electronics focus (Yen Phong, Que Vo, Gia Binh, Thuan Thanh).
2. **Bac Giang**: **Decision No. 219/QĐ-TTg** (17/02/2022)
   - *Target*: 29 Industrial Parks totaling ~7,000 ha; key anchor for global consumer electronics supply chains (Quang Chau, Van Trung, Viet Han, Yen Lu).
3. **Hai Phong**: **Decision No. 1516/QĐ-TTg** (02/12/2023)
   - *Target*: Establishment of South Hai Phong Coastal Economic Zone (20,000 ha) and expansion of modern maritime-linked industrial parks (Dinh Vu - Cat Hai, Deep C, Nam Cau Kien, Trang Due 3).
4. **Quang Ninh**: **Decision No. 80/QĐ-TTg** (11/02/2023)
   - *Target*: 23 Industrial Parks and clusters; massive expansion of Quang Yen Coastal Economic Zone (Song Khoai / Amata, Bac Tien Phong, Nam Tien Phong).
5. **Thai Nguyen**: **Decision No. 222/QĐ-TTg** (14/03/2023)
   - *Target*: 11 Industrial Parks and 41 industrial clusters; expansion of Yen Binh, Song Cong II, and Phu Binh mega-parks.
6. **Vinh Phuc**: **Decision No. 158/QĐ-TTg** (06/02/2024)
   - *Target*: 24 to 28 Industrial Parks (~7,000 ha); automotive, precision mechanical, and electronics clusters (Khai Quang, Ba Thien, Binh Xuyen, Tam Duong).
7. **Hung Yen**: **Decision No. 489/QĐ-TTg** (10/06/2024)
   - *Target*: 30 Industrial Parks (~9,589 ha); clean industrial corridor adjacent to Hanoi (Pho Noi A, Thang Long II, Yen My).
8. **Binh Duong**: **Decision No. 790/QĐ-TTg** (03/08/2024)
   - *Target*: 42 Industrial Parks (~21,000 ha); Pioneer of Eco-IP and Industry 4.0 conversions; expansion of VSIP III, Cay Truong, Bau Bang.
9. **Dong Nai**: **Decision No. 586/QĐ-TTg** (03/07/2024)
   - *Target*: 48 Industrial Parks (~22,000 ha); Aerotropolis industrial belt surrounding Long Thanh International Airport and Phuoc An deepwater port.
10. **Ba Ria - Vung Tau**: **Decision No. 1629/QĐ-TTg** (16/12/2023)
    - *Target*: 24 Industrial Parks (~16,052 ha); Heavy petrochemicals, green steel, offshore wind foundation fabrication, Cai Mep free trade zone.
11. **Long An**: **Decision No. 686/QĐ-TTg** (13/06/2023)
    - *Target*: 51 Industrial Parks (~12,433 ha); Gateway connecting HCMC and the Mekong Delta; high density of logistics and light manufacturing.
12. **Tay Ninh**: **Decision No. 173/QĐ-TTg** (29/12/2023)
    - *Target*: Expansion of Trang Bang, Thanh Thanh Cong, and establishment of Moc Bai border economic zone industrial parks.
13. **Quang Nam**: **Decision No. 72/QĐ-TTg** (17/01/2024)
    - *Target*: Chu Lai Open Economic Zone modernization, Thaco automotive mega-cluster, green multi-modal manufacturing.
14. **Quang Ngai**: **Decision No. 1456/QĐ-TTg** (22/11/2023)
    - *Target*: Dung Quat Economic Zone expansion, VSIP Quang Ngai, green metallurgical and chemical hubs.
15. **Thanh Hoa**: **Decision No. 153/QĐ-TTg** (27/02/2023)
    - *Target*: Nghi Son Economic Zone energy & petrochemical hub, Lam Son - Sao Vang high-tech industrial complex.

## 6. Energy Transition, Decarbonization & Grid Linkage

Industrial zones operating within the 2030–2050 horizon face mandatory carbon compliance (EU CBAM, US clean supply chain audits). The Vietnamese government has enacted statutory decrees aligning IP energy consumption with renewable targets:

### 6.1 National Power Development Plan VIII (PDP8)
* **Decision No. 500/QĐ-TTg (15/05/2023)** & Implementation Plan **Decision No. 262/QĐ-TTg (01/04/2024)**:
  - Establishes a total national capacity target of **150,489 MW by 2030**.
  - Directs dedicated 110kV/220kV transmission substation infrastructure directly to priority industrial corridors.

### 6.2 Direct Power Purchase Agreement (DPPA) Mechanism
* **Decree No. 80/2024/NĐ-CP (03/07/2024)**:
  - Authorizes large power consumers (>22kV connection voltage, monthly consumption $\ge 200,000\text{ kWh}$) inside industrial zones to procure clean electricity directly from renewable generators.
  - Two operational routes:
    1. **Private Line Route (Physical Connection)**: Directly connected via dedicated private line; zero tariff restrictions.
    2. **National Grid Route (Virtual / Contract-for-Difference)**: Transacted through the competitive wholesale electricity market (VWEM) with EVN wheeling charges.

### 6.3 Rooftop Solar Development in Industrial Zones
* **Decree No. 135/2024/NĐ-CP (22/10/2024)**:
  - Establishes mechanisms encouraging self-production and self-consumption (*tự sản xuất, tự tiêu thụ*) rooftop solar on industrial park factory roofs.
  - Zero capacity cap for self-consumed installations not connected to the national grid; simplified registration and fire safety protocols for grid-connected installations selling surplus up to 20% to EVN.

### 6.4 National Green Growth Strategy & Eco-Industrial Transition
* **Decision No. 1658/QĐ-TTg (01/10/2021)** & National Action Plan **Decision No. 882/QĐ-TTg (22/07/2022)**:
  - Targets at least 30% of operating industrial parks to transition toward Eco-Industrial Park standards by 2030.
