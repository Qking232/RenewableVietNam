1. Land Sublease & LURC (Sổ Đỏ) Month 1

Execution of definitive Sublease Agreement with Industrial Park Developer (50-year term). Verification of master zone master planning approval (Quy hoạch 1/2000), master ETP capacity connection, and handover of cadastral red line boundaries with clean site elevation benchmark.

2. 1/500 Master Plan & Basic Design (TKCS) Month 2

Compilation of detailed architectural site master plan (1/500) and Basic Design dossier (Thiết kế cơ sở) under Decree 15/2021/NĐ-CP. Verification of building coverage ratio (≤ 60%), internal traffic turning radiuses for 40ft container trucks (≥ 12.5m), and perimeter setbacks (≥ 6.0m).

Statutory Authority: Industrial Zone Management Board (BQL KCN) / Department of Construction (SXD).
3. Fire Design Appraisal (Thẩm Duyệt PCCC) Month 3.5

Submission of comprehensive fire engineering dossier to Police Department C07 under QCVN 06:2022/BXD and Circular 09/2023/TT-BXD. Specification of Promatect-H calcium silicate board encasement (R90/R120) for steel columns, Annex E.3 truss fireproofing exemption, and 1,200 m³ ESFR hydraulic reservoir.

Statutory Authority: Department of Fire Prevention, Fighting & Rescue (C07 Police).
4. Environmental License (Giấy Phép Môi Trường - GPMT) Month 3.5

Consolidated single license under Law on Environmental Protection 2020 (Decree 08/2022/NĐ-CP). Governing effluent discharge parameters (1,200 m³/day QCVN 40:2011 Column A), biomass boiler flue gas emissions (QCVN 19:2009 Class B), continuous emission monitoring system (CEMS), and hazardous waste storage.

Statutory Authority: Ministry of Natural Resources & Environment (BTNMT) / Provincial STNMT.
5. Phased Foundation Construction Permit (GPXD Móng) Month 4 (-45 Days)

Fast-track decoupled permitting approval allowing structural piling (1,850 spun piles D400/D500) and reinforced concrete pile caps to commence 45 to 60 days before the full superstructure architectural and MEP package is finalized.

Statutory Authority: Provincial Department of Construction (SXD) / BQL KCN.
6. State Handover & Ownership (Decree 06/2021) Month 14 (COD)

State inspection approval for completed construction (Nghiệm thu hoàn thành hạng mục công trình). Mandatory legal prerequisite for factory occupancy, issuance of Commercial Operation Date (COD), asset capitalization, and updating the Land Use Right Certificate (Hoàn công sở hữu nhà xưởng).

Statutory Authority: State Authority for Construction Quality Inspection (SACQI / Cục Giám định - BXD).

  • • Simulated Blackout: Intentional trip of EVN 22kV grid incomer. 2,000 kVA diesel generator auto-starts and assumes critical loads within 9.8 seconds via ATS.
