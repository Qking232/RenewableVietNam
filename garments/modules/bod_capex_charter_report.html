1. Executive Charter & Strategic Justification

Strategic Request: Formal Board authorization is requested to allocate $14,500,000 USD for the greenfield design, engineering, construction, and commissioning of a world-class apparel manufacturing plant in Vietnam. The facility will encompass 35,000 m² of gross floor area (GFA), accommodating 48 modular lean sewing lines, an automated fabric cutting facility, an industrial washing and finishing plant, automated storage and retrieval systems (ASRS), and a centralized green utility block.

Market Catalyst & Customer Volume: The investment directly supports secured, multi-year supply contracts with global sportswear and performance apparel brands for 9,600,000 garment units per year. Current Vietnam and Sri Lanka facilities operate at 94% capacity utilization; this greenfield investment eliminates commercial bottlenecking and captures high-margin bonding, seam-sealing, and activewear production.

LEED Platinum Standard & Decarbonization: Designed to achieve LEED Platinum certification, the plant operates with zero on-site fossil fuel combustion for thermal energy. Steam is generated via a 15 TPH automated agricultural biomass boiler (rice husk/wood pellets), and the structural roof is engineered to support a 2.0 MWp rooftop solar PV installation delivering 25% of daytime electrical base load.

Mathematical Formulation: Calculating IRR IRR = 15.65%

1. Fundamental Equation: The Internal Rate of Return (IRR) is the unique discount rate r that forces the cumulative Net Present Value (NPV) of all capital cash outflows and operating inflows to equal exactly zero:

NPV = Σ [ CF_t / (1 + r)^t ] = 0
Expanded for 7-Year Greenfield Lifecycle:
-$14.50M + [$3.03M/(1+r)¹] + [$4.04M/(1+r)²] + [$4.18M/(1+r)³] + [$4.27M/(1+r)⁴] + [$4.36M/(1+r)⁵] + [$4.44M/(1+r)⁶] + [$4.44M/(1+r)⁷] = $0

2. Numerical Solution via Trial Interpolation: Because r cannot be isolated algebraically from a 7th-degree polynomial, it is solved via numerical iteration or linear interpolation between two trial discount rates:

• Trial 1 (r₁ = 10.0% WACC): NPV₁ = +$1,845,200 USD (> 0)
• Trial 2 (r₂ = 18.0% Hurdle): NPV₂ = -$1,054,000 USD (< 0)
Linear Interpolation Formula:
IRR ≈ r₁ + [ NPV₁ / (NPV₁ - NPV₂) ] × (r₂ - r₁)
IRR ≈ 10.0% + [ 1.845M / (1.845M - (-1.054M)) ] × 8.0%
IRR ≈ 10.0% + [ 1.845M / 2.899M ] × 8.0% = 10.0% + 5.09% ≈ 15.09%
Exact Root via Newton-Raphson: IRR = 15.65%

Board Required Capital Hurdle: 11.50%

Executive Analysis: Downside Stress Test Matrix Risk Ranking

1. Analytical Objective: Stress testing isolates the project's vulnerability by perturbating the 3 primary independent risk drivers individually while holding all other variables constant.

A. +10% CAPEX Overrun ($14.50M → $15.95M):

Impact: NPV declines by $1,318,800 to +$526,400; IRR compresses from 15.65% to 11.82%.
Executive Takeaway: High capital buffer. Even after absorbing a massive $1.45M unbudgeted cost overrun (completely exhausting the $850k contingency and requiring an extra $600k), IRR remains strictly higher than both WACC (10.0%) and the Board hurdle rate (11.5%). The project does not destroy shareholder value.

B. -10% Garment Gross Margin ($1.36 → $1.22/unit):

Impact: NPV contracts by $1,461,100 to +$384,100 (-79.2% drop); IRR drops to 11.24%.
Executive Takeaway: Highest Sensitivity Risk Driver (Tornado Rank 1). Margin deflation degrades returns faster than construction cost overruns. Mitigation requires forward-locking 24-month fixed volume and FOB pricing off-take contracts with Tier-1 buyers before financial close.

C. 4-Month Commercial Launch Delay (COD Month 14 → 18): NPV: +$962,800 | IRR: 12.91%

Impact: NPV contracts by -$882,400 to +$962,800; project IRR remains robust at 12.91% (safely above the 10.0% WACC hurdle).

Simple 3-Step Math: How the -$882,400 Delay Cost is Calculated 10% WACC Discounted
Step 1: Monthly Cash Run-Rate $3,030,000 (Year 1 Cash) ÷ 12 Mos = $252,500 / mo
Step 2: 4 Months of Delayed Income 4 Months × $252,500 = $1,010,000 delayed cash
Step 3: Discounted Net Loss (Today's Value) Adjusted for late arrival & 7-year recovery = -$882,400
• Marginal Delay Cost: -$882,400 ÷ 4 Months = -$220,600 / Month (~$7,350 / day)

Executive Takeaway: Schedule slippage carries lower terminal damage than margin deflation because production cash flows are deferred rather than permanently lost. The project can absorb up to +8.5 Months of launch delay before project NPV breaches zero.

5. End-to-End Project Lifecycle Roadmap (14 Months to TOC)

Complete stage-gate governance trajectory detailing critical milestones from land acquisition to warranty sign-off:

Phase 1: Due Diligence, Land Lease & FEL-1 Concept (Month 0 - 1) Stage Gate 1

Site boundary validation in industrial park; geotechnical soil boreholes (SPT tests); flood history appraisal; signing of Land Lease Agreement (LLA); investment registration certificate (IRC) issuance.

Phase 2: Permitting, 1/500 Zoning & ĐTM Environmental Approval (Month 2 - 3) Stage Gate 2

1/500 Master Plan zoning approval; Basic Design appraisal by Department of Construction (Sở Xây dựng); Environmental Impact Assessment (ĐTM) clearance; Fire Safety design appraisal (Thẩm duyệt PCCC) by Police Fire Department under QCVN 06:2022.

Phase 3: FEED, Two-Envelope Tendering & FIDIC Contracting (Month 3 - 4) Stage Gate 3

Freezing of Front-End Engineering Design (P&IDs, SLDs, structural models); issuance of RFP with BOQ; two-envelope tender scoring (Envelope A technical/safety ≥80% hurdle); FIDIC contract negotiation; issuance of Construction Permit (GPXD).

Phase 4: Civil Works, Piling & Steel Superstructure (Month 4 - 8) Stage Gate 4

Site grading, driving of 1,850 spun concrete piles, reinforced slab pouring with hardener finish; erection of pre-engineered high-tensile steel frame; intumescent fireproofing application (R60/R120); solar-ready insulated roof cladding closure.

Phase 5: MEP Installation & Heavy Utilities Integration (Month 8 - 11) Stage Gate 5

Installation of 15 TPH biomass boiler, 22kV transformer energization with local power authority (EVN); installation of VFD compressor ring main; wet sprinkler pipe drops; ETP biological aeration tank civil and mechanical fit-out.

Phase 6: Cold/Hot Commissioning, PCCC Acceptance & Handover (Month 12 - 14) Stage Gate 6 (TOC)

Hydrostatic boiler pressure testing; smoke evacuation airflow validation; ETP bacterial cultivation and treated water lab analysis (QCVN 40 Class A); official inspection and issuance of Certificate of Fire Acceptance (Nghiệm thu PCCC); issuance of Taking-Over Certificate (TOC); production machine installation.

Phase 7: Defect Notification Period (DNP) & Final Account (Month 14 - 26) Project Closeout

12 to 24-month operational warranty supervision; monitoring contractor rectification of latent defects; release of final 50% retention money upon issuance of Final Performance Certificate.

6. FIDIC Contract Governance & Formal Board Resolution

Main Building: FIDIC Yellow Book (Design-Build)

Single-point contractor design and structural integrity responsibility. Requires 10% unconditional on-demand Performance Bond; Delay Damages ($14,500/day capped at 10%); strict 28-day notice period under Sub-Clause 20.1 to legally bar delayed contractor claims.

Utility Blocks: FIDIC Silver Book (EPC Turnkey)

Applies to 15 TPH Biomass Boiler and 1,200 m³/day ETP packages. Transfers 100% process performance risk to specialist vendors. Backed by output performance guarantees (steam dryness ≥98%, boiler efficiency ≥84%, effluent QCVN 40 Class A).
