Technical Fire Protection Engineering under QCVN 06:2022/BXD & Circular 09/2023/TT-BXD

Structural fire-resistance ratings (EI), compartmentation boundaries, and Circular 09/2023 regulatory exemptions:

A. Primary Structural Steel Columns (R90 / R120)
Building Occupancy: Manufacturing Hazard Category C (Hạng C)
Fire Resistance Rating: Level II (Bậc chịu lửa II)
Statutory Requirement: Load-bearing columns must achieve minimum R90
Selected Method: Calcium Silicate Board Encasement (15–20mm Promatect)
Why Board Encasement Replaced Intumescent Paint:

Under Circular 06/2022/TT-BCA, intumescent paint requires full-scale destructive furnace testing per paint batch at the Institute of Construction Science and Technology (IBST), leading to frequent 3–6 month testing bottlenecks. Calcium silicate dry boards hold pre-certified factory test reports, eliminating all batch-testing uncertainty.

B. Circular 09/2023/TT-BXD Roof Truss Exemption (R15 / R0)
Legacy QCVN 06:2021: Required R45 fire-retardant paint on all roof steel
Circular 09/2023/TT-BXD Amendment (Annex E.3): Prescriptive Exemption
Condition 1: Single-story Category C industrial building
Condition 2: 100% floor area protected by automatic ESFR ceiling sprinklers
Statutory Exemption Granted: Unprotected structural steel roof trusses permitted (R0)
Direct Project Impact:
• CAPEX Saving: -$320,000 USD (Eliminated 35,000 m² roof paint)
• Schedule Float Gained: +35 days of erection float
