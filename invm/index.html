VIETNAM INVESTMENT LEGAL MANAGEMENT & M&A ENGINE

6-Stage Statutory Legal & Investment Pipeline (Stage-Gate)

Standardized per Law on Investment 2020, Land Law 2024, Construction Law & PDP8.

Stage 1 0/6 thủ tục
CPM Critical Path & Bottlenecks: Tiến độ chuẩn

Project Origination & In-Principle Approval

Site survey, investment project proposal, DPI appraisal and In-Principle Investment Approval by Provincial PPC / Prime Minister.

Stage 2 0/10 thủ tục
CPM Critical Path & Bottlenecks: Tiến độ chuẩn

1/500 Detailed Master Plan & Basic Design (TKCS)

1/500 planning task & master plan approval, community consultation, and Basic Design (TKCS) appraisal by Department of Construction.

Stage 3 0/8 thủ tục
CPM Critical Path & Bottlenecks: Tiến độ chuẩn

Land Allocation, Site Clearance & Financial Settlement

Land allocation plan, land acquisition, specific land valuation under Land Law 2024, settlement of land fees, and LURC (Sổ Đỏ) issuance.

Stage 4 0/6 thủ tục
CPM Critical Path & Bottlenecks: Tiến độ chuẩn

Environmental (EIA) & Fire Safety (PCCC) Approvals

Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) approval, Environmental License (GPMT) and statutory Fire Safety design appraisal at C07/PC07.

Stage 5 0/5 thủ tục
CPM Critical Path & Bottlenecks: Tiến độ chuẩn

Detailed Construction Design & Building Permit (GPXD)

Detailed technical construction design (TKBVTC), independent structural safety check, Building Permit (GPXD) and site mobilization.

Stage 6 0/5 thủ tục
CPM Critical Path & Bottlenecks: Tiến độ chuẩn

Statutory Commissioning, Grid Connection & Handover (COD)

Fire safety acceptance, Decree 06/2021 State construction acceptance, utility interconnection and Commercial Operation Date (COD).
