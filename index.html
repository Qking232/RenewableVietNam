About us

A nationwide push toward renewable energy

Viet Nam has one of the fastest-growing energy needs in the region. Renewable Viet Nam is an information hub for that transition — a clear, plain-language place to understand where our clean energy comes from, what it powers, and how you can take part.

Reliable power

Helping the grid stay strong and dependable as more clean energy comes online across the country.

Cleaner air

Cutting reliance on coal and other fossil fuels means healthier communities and a safer climate for our children.

Local jobs

Building and maintaining solar and wind projects creates skilled work in provinces across Viet Nam.

Lower costs

Solar and wind are now among the cheapest sources of new electricity, easing pressure on household bills.

Focus areas

Where Viet Nam's clean energy comes from

Four main sources do the heavy lifting — each one abundant, clean and made in Viet Nam.

Solar power

Rooftop and utility-scale panels soak up Viet Nam's strong year-round sunshine, especially in the sunny south and central regions.

Wind power

Coastal and offshore wind along the long coastline turns steady sea breezes into large amounts of clean electricity.

Hydropower

Rivers and reservoirs have powered Viet Nam for decades and remain a backbone of our clean electricity supply.

Storage & grid

Batteries and smarter transmission keep the lights on when the sun sets or the wind drops, making clean power reliable.
