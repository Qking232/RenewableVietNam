A

Corridor A: Direct Dedicated Line (Đường Dây Riêng)

Decree 80/2024 Chapter II (Articles 4 – 10)
  • • 0% EVN Transmission Charge: Direct private interconnection bypasses national grid tariff structures.
  • • Physical Siting Boundary: Generator and factory must be situated within dedicated industrial boundaries or ≤ 10km proximity.
  • • 100% Clean Green Attribute: Zero grid dilution, direct I-REC issuance, fully verified for CBAM & RE100.
B

Corridor B: National Grid Wheeling (Qua Lưới Điện Quốc Gia)

Decree 80/2024 Chapter III (Articles 11 – 20)
  • • Wholesale Electricity Market (CGM): Energy settled via the spot market with synthetic Contract-for-Differences (CfD) hedge.
  • • Cross-Regional Wheeling: Allows factories in Northern / Southern hubs to contract utility wind/solar in Central Vietnam.
  • • Intertie Requirement: Consumer must connect at ≥ 22kV voltage level with minimum 200,000 kWh/month consumption.
